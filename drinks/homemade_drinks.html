Easy Homemade Drinks Reference

Simple, cheap, low-sugar-waste versions of sun tea, cold brew tea, and Kool-Aid.

Sun Tea

Ratio (1-gallon pitcher, syrup included): 15 cups cold water + 4–6 family-size tea bags (or 8–10 regular bags)

  1. Fill your pitcher/jar with 15 cups cold water (holding back 1 cup for syrup), drop in tea bags.
  2. Let sit at room temp or in sunlight 3–5 hours. Don't go past ~8 hours — can turn bitter/funky.
  3. Remove bags. Stir in 1 cup simple syrup (see below). Total lands right around 1 gallon.

Cold Brew Tea

Ratio (1-gallon pitcher, syrup included): 15 cups cold water + 4–6 family-size tea bags (or 8–10 regular bags)

  1. Fill your pitcher with 15 cups cold water (holding back 1 cup for syrup), drop in bags, cover.
  2. Refrigerate 6–12 hours (overnight is easiest). More forgiving than sun tea — won't go bitter if it sits a bit longer.
  3. Remove bags. Stir in 1 cup simple syrup (see below). Total lands right around 1 gallon.

Kool-Aid (Really Sweet, Less Sugar Used)

Base: 1 packet Kool-Aid per 2 quarts water (check your packet — some make more)

Ratio (1-gallon pitcher, syrup included): 13 cups cold water + Kool-Aid packet(s)

  1. Mix Kool-Aid packet(s) into 13 cups cold water (holding back room for a bigger syrup batch — Kool-Aid needs more sugar than tea to taste "really sweet").
  2. Stir in your Kool-Aid syrup (2 cups sugar + 2 cups hot water, cooled — see below). This brings you close to a gallon.
  3. Taste and adjust: top off with a splash more cold water if too strong, or add more syrup in small splashes if you want it sweeter.

Pick one approach if you want to cut sugar further:

  • Sweetened packets ("no sugar needed" kind) — just add water, no scooping sugar.
  • Less water than called for — stronger/sweeter per cup without more sugar; stretch with extra ice when serving.
  • Half sugar, half Splenda/stevia — cuts real sugar usage roughly in half, still tastes fully sweet (not "diet" flavor). Best long-term sugar-saving option.
  • Simple syrup instead of raw sugar — dissolves fully and evenly, so you're not over-scooping to compensate for gritty undissolved sugar.

Simple Syrup (for cold drinks)

Use for cold brew tea or Kool-Aid when you want sugar to dissolve evenly and use less overall. Always equal parts sugar + hot water — amount just changes by drink.

  • For 1 gallon sweet tea (sun tea or cold brew): 1 cup sugar + 1 cup hot water
  • For 1 gallon "really sweet" Kool-Aid: 2 cups sugar + 2 cups hot water (adjust to taste)
  1. Stir sugar and hot water together until fully clear/dissolved.
  2. Cool, then stir into your cold pitcher.
  3. Store extra in the fridge (keeps a few weeks) — makes sweetening future batches faster, and lets you top off sweetness without a whole new pot.

General Sugar-Saving Tips

  • Brew tea stronger (more bags) so you need less sugar to make it taste "full" — weak tea gets oversweetened just to give it flavor.
  • Always dissolve sugar before adding to cold drinks (syrup or warm-tea method) — undissolved sugar sinks and tempts you to add more.
  • Half sugar / half sweetener works well across all three drinks if you want to cut sugar spend without losing sweetness.
