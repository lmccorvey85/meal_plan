Big-Batch Velveted Teriyaki Chicken Grain Bowls

← All Recipes  |  Lunch

Meal PrepServes 5

Overview

Velveted chicken thighs, frozen stir-fry vegetables, Success Rice, and a double-batch teriyaki sauce, portioned into 5 grab-and-go bowls.

Ingredients — Chicken & Velveting Marinade

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (or low-sodium)
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/8 tsp baking soda

Ingredients — Vegetables & Rice

  • 1 bag (12–16 oz) frozen stir-fry vegetable blend
  • 2 regular Success Rice bags (yields ~5 cups cooked; check box yield, size up to family-size if needed)

Ingredients — Teriyaki Sauce (double batch)

  • 3/4 cup bottled teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water, whisked separately (slurry)
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder (or 1 clove minced garlic)
  • 1 tbsp oil, for searing

Prep

  1. Cut chicken into bite-size (roughly 1-inch) pieces.
  2. Combine chicken with velveting marinade ingredients; toss until evenly coated.
  3. Marinate 15–20 min at room temp, or refrigerate a few hours ahead.
  4. Whisk the cornstarch-water slurry separately — keep it out of the sauce until the final step.
  5. Mix teriyaki sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic powder in a bowl (slurry stays separate).

Cooking

  1. Cook Success Rice per package directions; set aside, keep warm.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high until shimmering.
  3. Sear chicken in batches — don't overcrowd, or it steams instead of sears. 2–3 min per batch until edges turn opaque and lightly golden. Remove each batch.
  4. In the same pan, add frozen stir-fry vegetables. Cook 4–5 min over medium-high, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp.
  5. Return all chicken to the pan. Pour in the teriyaki sauce mixture, stir to coat.
  6. Re-whisk the cornstarch slurry (it settles), pour it in. Simmer 2–3 min, stirring, until the sauce thickens and the chicken reaches 165°F internal.
  7. Remove from heat, cool 5–10 min before portioning so containers don't steam/sweat in the fridge.

Portioning

Divide rice evenly among 5 containers, top with chicken, veggies, and sauce. Seal and refrigerate.

Containers

5–6 32 oz (4-cup) rectangular meal-prep containers, microwave-safe with vented lids preferred. Glass (Pyrex/Rubbermaid Brilliance) is a good long-term upgrade.

Storage & Reheating

Fridge: good for 4–5 days in airtight containers. Reheat microwave-covered, 1.5–2 min, stirring halfway. Add a splash of water if the rice looks dry.

Shortcut Options

Rotisserie chicken instead of raw thighs — skip velveting/searing, shred and toss in sauce. Faster, but less juicy. Bottled teriyaki sauce doctored with soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, and ginger.

Estimated Cost

~$14–18 total for 5 servings (~$3/serving).
