5-Day Overnight Oats Meal Prep

← All Recipes  |  Breakfast

Meal PrepGrab and goServes 5

Overview

One base recipe, five flavor variations — make all five jars in one sitting on Sunday and grab-and-eat cold all week. No reheating needed.

Jars Needed

5 pint-size (16 oz) wide-mouth mason jars. Size up to 24 oz jars for fuller-flavor days (apple cinnamon, PB banana).

Base Recipe (per jar)

  • 1/2 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vanilla or plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp chia seeds (optional, helps thicken)

Flavor Mix-Ins by Day

DayAdd to base
Mon — Cinnamon brown sugar1 tsp cinnamon + 1 tbsp brown sugar + 1/4 tsp vanilla
Tue — Peanut butter banana1 tbsp peanut butter + 1/2 banana, sliced
Wed — Apple cinnamon1/4 cup diced apple + 1 tsp cinnamon + 1 tsp maple syrup
Thu — Chocolate PB1 tbsp cocoa powder + 1 tbsp peanut butter + 1 tbsp honey
Fri — Berry1/4 cup frozen mixed berries (thawed)

Instructions

  1. In each jar, add oats, milk, and yogurt. Stir well to combine.
  2. Add the day's mix-ins and stir again.
  3. Lid each jar, refrigerate overnight (at least 4 hours, ideally overnight).
  4. Grab and eat cold straight from the jar. Keeps well in the fridge up to 5 days.

Shortcut Options

Single-serve peanut butter cups instead of a jar, if you want to skip the scooping/measuring on Tue and Thu.

Estimated Cost

~$14–18 total for 5 servings (~$3/serving), less if you already have pantry staples like brown sugar, cocoa powder, or maple syrup on hand.
