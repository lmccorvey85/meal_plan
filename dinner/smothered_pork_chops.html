Southern Smothered Pork Chops with Onion Pan Gravy, Doctored Mashed Potatoes & Honey-Butter Green Beans

← All Recipes  |  Dinner

Standard DinnerServes 8Active ~1 hr 20 minTotal ~1 hr 30–40 min

Check Before Buying (pantry staples you likely have)

  • Sea salt, black pepper
  • Garlic powder, onion powder
  • Smoked paprika
  • All-purpose flour
  • Vegetable/canola oil
  • Butter (need ~14 tbsp / 1¾ sticks total across all components)
  • Brown sugar
  • A Cajun/Southern seasoning blend (Tony Chachere's, Slap Ya Mama, etc.)

Ingredients

Pork Chops

  • 8 bone-in pork chops, ~7–8 oz each (3.7–4 lbs total)
  • 1 tbsp sea salt (go light — the Cajun blend adds salt too)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1½ tsp onion powder
  • 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp Cajun/Southern seasoning blend
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour (for dredging)
  • 3–4 tbsp vegetable oil (for searing, divided across batches)

Onion Pan Gravy (doubled)

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 3 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 packets brown gravy mix (McCormick or similar)
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper, ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1–2 tbsp brown sugar

Doctored Mashed Potatoes

  • 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth (or water)
  • 1 cup whole milk or half-and-half
  • 8 tbsp (1 stick) butter
  • 4–4½ cups instant potato flakes
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder
  • Salt & pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar (optional)

Honey-Butter Green Beans

  • 2 lbs frozen cut green beans (or 3 cans, 14.5 oz each, drained)
  • 4 tbsp butter, 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Equipment

  • 12" cast iron skillet (searing)
  • Dutch oven (onions + gravy + smothering)
  • Large pot or skillet (green beans)
  • Saucepan (potatoes)
  • Meat thermometer, tongs, whisk

Cooking Timeline (~1 hr 30–40 min total)

Times are relative to T-0:00 = start of prep. Two burners run in parallel (cast iron + Dutch oven), so read across.

1:15Plate & serve — chop with gravy, mashed potatoes, honey-butter green beans.

Visual Doneness Cues

  • Searing chops: deep golden-brown, releases easily from the pan when ready to flip (don't force it early).
  • Caramelized onions: soft, jammy, deep amber-brown, not just translucent — takes real time, don't rush past medium-low.
  • Gravy: coats the back of a spoon, no raw flour/packet taste.
  • Pork chops: 145°F internal (thickest part, away from bone) is the USDA safe minimum for pork — yields a hint of pink and full juiciness. Always rest 3–5 min after pulling; temp carries over slightly.
  • Mashed potatoes: smooth, no lumps, holds soft peaks on a spoon.
  • Green beans: bright green, glossy from the glaze, tender but with a slight bite.

Storage

  • Pork chops: store submerged in their gravy in an airtight container — don't separate chop from gravy, it protects the meat from drying/re-firming. Fridge up to 4 days, freeze in gravy up to 3 months.
  • Mashed potatoes: separate airtight container, fridge up to 4 days.
  • Green beans: separate airtight container, fridge up to 4 days.

Reheating

  • Chops + gravy: covered skillet or saucepan over low heat, spooning gravy over as it warms, 8–10 min, until 165°F. Avoid high-power microwaving — toughens the meat; if you must, use 50% power in short bursts, chop submerged in gravy.
  • Mashed potatoes: saucepan over low with a splash of milk, stirring, or microwave covered in 30-sec bursts, stirring between.
  • Green beans: skillet over medium-low with a small pat of butter, 2–3 min, tossing.

Shortcut vs. From-Scratch

ComponentShortcut (this recipe)From-Scratch AlternativeTrade-off
GravyBrown gravy packets + caramelized onions + brothRoux from pork drippings, no packetsShortcut saves 10–15 min and guarantees consistent thickness; scratch roux is deeper/more complex but needs more attention
PotatoesInstant flakes doctored with butter, dairy, sour cream, cheesePeeled, boiled, hand-mashed russets/Yukon goldsShortcut cuts 30–40 min; doctored flakes are hard to distinguish once loaded with fat/dairy. Scratch has better texture for potato purists
Green beansFrozen/canned, steamed and glazedFresh, trimmed, blanched, then glazedShortcut saves ~15 min prep and is fully reasonable for weeknight scale; fresh beans have a slightly firmer bite and brighter flavor
