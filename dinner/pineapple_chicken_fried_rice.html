Better Golden Pineapple Butter-Washed Chicken Fried Rice

← All Recipes  |  Dinner

Standard DinnerSouthern-Asian fusion

Overview

Pineapple chicken fried rice built on juicy butter-soy-cornstarch marinated chicken thighs. Serve with at least one vegetable side (roasted broccoli, asparagus, Brussels sprouts, doctored green beans, or honey-butter carrots) — the vegetable is not baked into the main dish itself.

Key Ingredients

  • Boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Cold, day-old jasmine or long-grain rice (or cooked-ahead, chilled Success Rice)
  • Pineapple chunks, drained and seared before adding (prevents mushy rice)
  • Golden raisins
  • Eggs, onion, garlic, peas and carrots, scallions
  • Curry powder, soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil
  • Optional: lime or rice vinegar

Sauce Notes

Use a generous double sauce amount, added gradually and tasted as you go. Drain and sear the pineapple separately before folding it in — this keeps the rice from turning mushy.

Rice Notes

Success boil-in-bag rice works, but cook it ahead and chill it first — fried rice needs cold, dried-out rice to fry properly instead of steaming into a mush.

Shortcut Options

  • Rotisserie chicken instead of raw thighs
  • Success Rice, cooked ahead and chilled
  • Frozen peas and carrots
  • Canned pineapple
  • Pre-chopped onion

Notes
