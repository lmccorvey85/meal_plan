Sunday Juicy Velveted Chicken Stir Fry with Success Rice and Honey-Butter Carrots

← All Recipes  |  Dinner

Standard DinnerWok stir fry

Overview

Juicy velveted chicken stir fry cooked in a hot 14" carbon steel wok, served over Success Rice with honey-butter carrots on the side. Frozen stir fry vegetable bags go straight into the main dish.

Key Ingredients

  • Boneless, skinless chicken thighs, sliced thin
  • Frozen stir fry vegetable blend
  • Success Rice
  • Carrots, butter, honey (for the side)
  • Soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, cornstarch
  • Garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, ginger, brown sugar, vinegar
  • Optional: green onions

Chicken Velveting Method

Slice chicken thin, then velvet it with soy sauce, cornstarch, oil, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and a small amount of baking soda (optional brown sugar). Cook in batches in a hot wok so it sears instead of steaming — don't overcrowd the pan.

Sauce: generous double sauce amount, but add about 75% first and adjust to taste before adding the rest.

Salt-reduction notes (from last cook — it ran too salty):
  • Use low-sodium soy sauce throughout.
  • For 4 lb chicken: marinade with 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce + 1 Tbsp water.
  • Stir fry sauce: reduce soy sauce to about 2/3 cup low-sodium.
  • Increase unsalted chicken broth or water to about 1¼ cups.
  • Reduce oyster sauce to 1/3 cup.
  • Skip chicken bouillon powder unless the sauce tastes flat after thickening.
  • Don't salt the Success Rice unless it needs it after tasting.
  • Reduce honey-butter carrot salt to 1/2 tsp.

Rice Notes

For 8 servings: about 8 cups cooked rice, usually 4 regular Success Rice bags or 2 family-size bags. Check the box yield — sizes vary.

Shortcut Options

  • Frozen stir fry vegetable bags instead of hand-cut veg
  • Success Rice
  • Bottled stir fry sauce, doctored with broth/water, sesame oil, butter, garlic powder, ginger, and a small splash of vinegar

Storage & Reheating

Store rice separately from the stir fry. Store chicken and vegetables with plenty of sauce. Reheat gently, covered, with a splash of water or broth to keep the chicken juicy.
