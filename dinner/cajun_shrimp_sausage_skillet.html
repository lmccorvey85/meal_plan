Cajun Shrimp & Sausage Skillet with Buttered Garlic Green Beans over Success Rice

← All Recipes  |  Dinner

Quick WeeknightMeal Prep – Serves 6Active ~45 minTotal ~55 minMild-medium spice

Meal-prep, extra-saucy batch (scaled from the original 4-serving version): scaled up to 6 servings, and the sauce was doubled again on top of the recipe's usual "double sauce" rule — the first batch's sauce ran thin and didn't fully coat the rice. This version makes noticeably more pan sauce.

Check Before Buying (pantry staples you likely have)

  • Butter, olive oil
  • Cajun seasoning, smoked paprika, black pepper
  • Chicken broth (low-sodium)
  • Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce

Ingredients

Protein & Trinity

  • 2¼ lb large shrimp, peeled/deveined, tails off
  • 1¼ lb (~20 oz) andouille sausage, sliced into ½" coins (2 packages)
  • 1½ cups diced yellow onion
  • 1½ cups diced green bell pepper
  • 1 cup + 2 Tbsp diced celery
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, minced (for the skillet)
  • 1½ tsp Cajun seasoning, for tossing the shrimp

Sauce (doubled again — build to ~75% first, taste, then finish)

  • 1 Tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1½ tsp black pepper
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 cans (10 oz each) diced tomatoes with green chiles (Rotel), drained slightly
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Hot sauce, to taste
  • 9 Tbsp butter, divided (3 Tbsp for sautéing the trinity, 6 Tbsp for finishing the sauce)
  • 1½ Tbsp olive oil, for searing sausage
  • 3 green onions, sliced, for garnish

Green Beans

  • 3 cans (14.5 oz) green beans, drained (or ~18 oz frozen)
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Scant ½ tsp Cajun seasoning
  • Splash of chicken broth

Rice

  • Success Rice — need ~6 cups cooked rice, about 3 regular bags (or 1 family-size + 1 regular). Check box yield, sizes vary.

Equipment

  • Large (12"+) cast iron or heavy skillet — the bigger batch needs the room; a Dutch oven works too
  • Medium saucepan (green beans)
  • Pot or microwave for rice
  • Tongs, cutting board/knife

Prep

  1. Pat shrimp dry; toss with 1½ tsp Cajun seasoning. Refrigerate until ready to cook.
  2. Slice sausage into ½" coins.
  3. Dice onion, bell pepper, celery (medium dice, roughly even size). Mince garlic (skillet and green bean portions separately).
  4. Drain green beans and the diced tomatoes.
  5. Start rice per package directions so it's ready when the skillet is.

Cooking Instructions

1. Sear the sausage (6–7 min). Heat 1½ Tbsp oil in the skillet over medium-high. Sear sausage in a single layer — work in two batches if it crowds the pan — 2–3 min per side until browned. Remove to a plate.

2. Cook the trinity (5–6 min). Lower to medium. Add 3 Tbsp butter to the same skillet. Add onion, bell pepper, celery; sauté, stirring occasionally, 4–5 min until softened and onion is translucent. Add garlic; cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

3. Build the sauce (5–6 min). Stir in Cajun seasoning, smoked paprika, black pepper. Add the broth, drained tomatoes, and Worcestershire. Bring to a simmer over medium. Build to about 75% of the liquid first, taste, then add the rest and adjust seasoning/hot sauce — this is a big batch of sauce, so give it a full simmer, 5–6 min, to let the flavors come together before the shrimp go in.

4. Return sausage, add shrimp in batches (5–6 min). Return sausage to the skillet. Nestle shrimp into the sauce — if it's a tight fit, add shrimp in two batches so they sit in the sauce rather than stack. Cook 2 min, flip, cook 1–2 min more per batch until shrimp are opaque and just curled into a "C" shape (not a tight "O" — that means overcooked). Cook to 145°F if checking with a thermometer; don't rely on carryover to finish them — shrimp are too thin to carry over 20+ degrees. Remove from heat as soon as they're done.

5. Finish. Stir in the remaining 6 Tbsp butter off-heat until glossy. Garnish with green onions.

Green beans (run in parallel, start around step 2): Melt 3 Tbsp butter in a saucepan over medium. Add garlic, cook 30 sec. Add green beans and a splash of broth. Season with Cajun seasoning. Simmer uncovered 6–8 min, stirring occasionally, until heated through and lightly glazed.

Keeping Warm

If rice finishes early, keep covered off heat with a pat of butter. Keep the skillet on low with the lid ajar for up to 10 min — don't let the shrimp sit on direct heat or they'll toughen.

Visual Doneness Cues

  • Sausage: deep golden-brown sear on both cut sides.
  • Trinity: onion translucent, pepper and celery softened but not mushy.
  • Sauce: simmering gently, slightly reduced, coats the back of a spoon.
  • Shrimp: opaque pink, curled into a loose "C" (a tight "O" means overcooked). 145°F internal if checking with a thermometer.
  • Green beans: bright green, glossy from the butter, tender with a slight bite.

Storage (Meal Prep)

  • Cool completely, then portion into 6 individual airtight containers — skillet mixture (with plenty of sauce spooned over), rice, and green beans can go in the same container or separate compartments, your call.
  • Fridge up to 3–4 days. Shrimp-based dishes don't freeze well (texture suffers) — fridge only recommended.

Reheating

  • Skillet mixture: covered pan over medium-low with a splash of broth, just until warmed through — avoid boiling, which toughens the shrimp. Microwave option: 50% power, covered, stirring halfway.
  • Rice: splash of water or broth, covered, microwave or stovetop.
  • Green beans: quick sauté reheat or microwave.
