Smoked Sausage, Cabbage & Potato Stew with Cinnamon-Brown Sugar, Buttermilk Cornbread and Doctored Green Beans

← All Recipes  |  Dinner

Full FlavorSlow cookerSouthern

Overview

A thick, stew-consistency (not brothy) slow cooker dish — smoked sausage, cabbage, and potatoes with a Southern-seasoned, cinnamon-brown-sugar undertone. Served with upgraded boxed buttermilk cornbread and doubled doctored canned green beans.

Key Ingredients

  • Smoked sausage or kielbasa
  • Green cabbage, Yukon gold potatoes (cut into 1.5" cubes)
  • Onion, celery, carrots, garlic
  • Low-sodium chicken broth, diced tomatoes
  • Smoked paprika, thyme, bay leaf, Tony Chachere's, garlic powder, onion powder
  • Brown sugar, ground cinnamon, cornstarch (thickener)
  • Bacon
  • Canned green beans, Jiffy corn muffin mix, buttermilk

Seasoning & Technique Notes

This is a revised, stew-thickened version of an earlier soup that came out under-seasoned. Changes made:

  • Increased Tony Chachere's from 1 tsp to 2 tsp; smoked paprika from 2 tsp to 1 Tbsp; thyme from 1 tsp to 1½ tsp.
  • Added 1 tsp garlic powder + 1 tsp onion powder.
  • Added 2 Tbsp brown sugar + ½ tsp ground cinnamon.
  • Removed apple cider vinegar entirely (disliked in the original).
  • Converted from soup to stew: broth reduced from 6 cups to 2.5–3 cups; stirred in a 2 Tbsp cornstarch + 2 Tbsp water slurry during the last 30–45 min; finished uncovered on HIGH to thicken/reduce.

Season with salt/Tony Chachere's at the end of cooking, not the start — broth and tomato sodium varies. Optional: sear a portion of the cabbage in the sausage drippings before it goes in the slow cooker for deeper caramelized flavor.

Cabbage overflow technique: if all the cabbage doesn't fit at once, add the first ~60% at the start, let it cook down 1.5–2 hrs on LOW (or ~1 hr on HIGH), then stir in the reserved cabbage submerged in the liquid. Count total cook time from when the first batch went in.

Rice Note

Not used in this recipe — potatoes and cornbread already cover the starch, and rice would compete rather than complement. If added anyway: Success Rice, plain/buttered, about 4 cups cooked for 8 servings (2 regular bags), frozen separately from the stew.

Shortcut Options

  • Pre-chopped bagged cabbage (not coleslaw mix — too finely cut)
  • Jarred minced garlic
  • Doctored boxed Jiffy cornbread mix (buttermilk swap + honey + extra butter)
  • Canned green beans doctored with bacon, onion, garlic, and bouillon

Notes

Sides (cornbread, green beans) were great as-is except the green beans needed doubling for 8 servings. Taste-test the next cook to confirm the cinnamon/brown sugar reads as "warm," not "sweet" — if too sweet, cut back to 1 Tbsp brown sugar / ¼ tsp cinnamon.
